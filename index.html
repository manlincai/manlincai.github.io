Welcome! My name is Manlin Cai. I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Sociology at the University of British Columbia.

My scholarship examines how workplace institutions and digital technologies shape inequality across work and family life, connecting economic sociology, social stratification, and technology and society. My research has followed two major lines of inquiry. First, I use quantitative methods to examine gender inequality in workplace power and status, as well as its implications for family life. My second line of research foregrounds how digital technology shapes intimate relationships and social integration among racialized immigrants. Leveraging both quantitative and qualitative methods, my research analyzes large-scale nationally representative datasets alongside in-depth interviews. I have 12 peer-reviewed articles forthcoming or published in leading journals, including Social Forces, Work and Occupations and Canadian Review of Sociology.

I received my Ph.D. in Sociology from the University of British Columbia in 2026, M.A. in Social Sciences (Sociology) from the University of Chicago in 2019, and B.A. in Economics from Peking University in 2018.
